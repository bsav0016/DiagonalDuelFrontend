Delete your Diagonal Duel account

Applies to the Diagonal Duel app on iPhone and Android.

In the app

  1. Sign in to the app.
  2. Open Profile.
  3. Choose Delete Account, confirm, and enter your password.

The account is deleted straight away.

If you cannot use the app

Email savidgeapps@gmail.com from the address on your account, with the subject "Delete my Diagonal Duel account", and we will delete it for you. Please include your username.

What is removed

  • Your username and email address are replaced with anonymous values, and your password is turned off, so nobody can sign in to the account again.
  • You are removed from matchmaking, and your push notification tokens are deleted.
  • Your open challenges are cancelled and your records of games against the computer are deleted.
  • Your computer points are set to zero, so you no longer appear on the leaderboards.
  • Games still in progress end as a forfeit.

What is kept

Finished games stay so that your opponents keep their game history. They show only the anonymous name, not your username or email. Nothing else about you is kept.

More detail is in the privacy policy.
