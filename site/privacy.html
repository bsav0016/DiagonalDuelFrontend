Diagonal Duel Privacy Policy

Effective 20 November 2026

Diagonal Duel is made by Savidge Apps ("we", "us"). This page says what the game stores, why, who else handles it, and how to delete it. We have no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and we do not sell your data.

Without an account

You can play the computer, pass the phone to a friend and solve the daily puzzle with no account. Your settings and your puzzle progress and streak stay on your phone, and nothing about them is sent to us.

With an account

An account is needed to play online, send or answer challenges and appear on the leaderboards. We store:

  • Username and email address. The username is shown to other players. The email is used only to send you a password reset code, and is never shown to anyone.
  • Password. Stored only in scrambled (hashed) form, so we cannot read it.
  • Games. Who played, every move, the result and the rating change. Your opponent can see the moves of a game you played with them.
  • Challenges. A challenge you send has a random invite code in its link. Anyone who opens the link while it is open can answer it, so share it only with the person you mean.
  • Leaderboards. Your username, online rating and computer points are shown to other players.
  • Games against the computer while signed in. The moves and the result are sent to the server so that wins can be checked before they earn points. These records are kept with your account and deleted when you delete it.

Notifications (optional)

If you allow notifications, the app registers a push token for your phone, with the platform (iPhone), on our server. We use it to tell you when it is your turn, when a time limit is running out, and about challenges. The messages are delivered through Expo's push service and Apple's notification service. You can turn notifications off in your phone's settings, and signing out removes the token.

Password reset

If you ask to reset your password, a six-digit code is emailed to the address on your account (sent through Gmail). We store the code only in scrambled form and it stops working after a short time.

App updates

To fix problems without waiting for an app store review, the app asks Expo's update service (EAS Update) each time it opens whether a newer version of the app's code is available. That request carries what any internet request carries, such as your IP address, plus the app version and your device's platform. Your account, games and puzzle progress are not part of it, and we do not use it to identify anyone. Expo's own policy applies to how it handles those requests: expo.dev/privacy.

Who else handles your data

  • Heroku hosts our server and database, in the United States.
  • Expo provides the push service and the update service described above.
  • Apple delivers push notifications to iPhones.
  • Google (Gmail) sends the password reset email.

They handle data only to provide those services for us. We do not use analytics, advertising or crash-reporting services.

Deleting your data

In the app, open Profile and choose Delete Account (it asks for your password). We then remove you from matchmaking, remove your push tokens, cancel your open challenges, delete your records of games against the computer, set your computer points to zero, replace your username and email with anonymous values, and turn off your password. Games in progress end as a forfeit. Finished games stay so that your opponents keep their history, but they show only the anonymous name.

If you cannot use the app, email us from the address on your account and we will do the same.

Children

Diagonal Duel is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you think a child has made an account, email us and we will delete it.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date above will change. The full history of changes is public in the repository.
