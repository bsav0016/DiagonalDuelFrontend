Diagonal Duel

Four in a row on a diamond board. Take turns, build outward from the corner, and be the first to line up four.

Get it on the App Store

Free on iPhone and iPad.

A different kind of four in a row

The board is an 8x8 diamond and a piece can only go next to the pieces already in play, so every move opens some lines and closes others. Not sure where you can play? Show Valid Moves lights up every legal square.

Play however you like

  • Challenge a friend: send an invite link or a code, choose how long each move gets, and play at your own pace. Rematch when it ends.
  • Play the computer at any level from 0 to 100, from a friendly opponent to one that thinks several moves ahead.
  • Pass and play: two players on one phone, no account needed.
  • Play online against other players. Games are asynchronous, with a time limit on each move, so nobody has to be online at the same time. Climb the leaderboard.

Easy to learn

The rules fit in one sentence, and there is a short How to Play screen in the app.
